Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Sri Laxmi Home Foods respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and how we protect it when you use our website and food-ordering services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide while creating an account, placing an order, submitting a contact form or communicating with us.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • House number, street, area, landmark and delivery address
  • Hyderabad city and pincode
  • Food items, quantity and order details
  • Selected payment method
  • Messages submitted through the Contact page
  • Location coordinates when you voluntarily allow location access

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing customer accounts
  • Processing food orders
  • Preparing and delivering orders
  • Confirming customer and delivery details
  • Providing customer support
  • Responding to enquiries and complaints
  • Managing cancellations and refunds
  • Improving website performance and security
  • Preventing misuse or fraudulent activity
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations

3. Location Information

Location access is optional. We collect latitude and longitude only when you select the “Get Current Location” button and allow location permission through your browser.

Location information may be used to assist with food delivery. You may also enter your address manually without sharing your current location.

4. Browser Storage

Our website may use browser local storage to temporarily store:

  • Login session information
  • Basic account information
  • Shopping-cart items
  • Recent order information

You may clear this information through your browser settings. Clearing browser data may sign you out and remove items saved in your shopping cart.

5. Sharing of Information

Sri Laxmi Home Foods does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Persons responsible for preparing orders
  • Delivery personnel
  • Website hosting providers
  • Cloud database and storage providers
  • Payment providers when online payments are introduced
  • Email, SMS or messaging providers when customer notification services are enabled
  • Government or legal authorities when required by applicable law

6. Payment Information

Cash on Delivery is currently available. When online payments are introduced, transactions may be processed by an authorised third-party payment provider.

Sri Laxmi Home Foods will not request or store complete debit-card or credit-card numbers, CVV details, UPI PINs, bank passwords or OTPs.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect customer information. However, no website, internet transmission, database or server can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should protect their account credentials and immediately report suspected unauthorised access.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to process orders, provide customer support, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted, anonymised or securely archived.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request:

  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Information about personal data held by us
  • Deletion of eligible personal information
  • Withdrawal of optional location permission
  • Assistance with privacy-related concerns

Some order and transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Children should use this website only with the knowledge and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

A parent or guardian may contact us regarding information submitted by a child.

11. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Sri Laxmi Home Foods is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites and services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, business operations or legal requirements change.

The latest version and updated date will be displayed on this page.

13. Contact Us

For questions, corrections, deletion requests or privacy concerns, contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods through our Contact page.
