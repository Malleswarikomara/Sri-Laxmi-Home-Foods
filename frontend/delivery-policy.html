Delivery Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This Delivery Policy explains the delivery areas, address requirements, estimated delivery times and customer responsibilities for food orders placed through Sri Laxmi Home Foods.

1. Delivery Service Area

Sri Laxmi Home Foods currently provides food delivery only within supported areas of Hyderabad.

Orders outside the supported Hyderabad delivery area may be rejected or cancelled.

2. Supported Pincodes

Customers must enter a valid six-digit Hyderabad pincode while placing an order.

The website currently accepts Hyderabad pincodes beginning with:

  • 500xxx

Acceptance of a pincode does not automatically guarantee delivery when the exact location is outside the active service range.

3. Delivery Address Requirements

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address containing:

  • House or flat number
  • Street or road name
  • Area or locality
  • Nearby landmark, when available
  • City
  • Six-digit pincode
  • Valid mobile number

Customers should verify all address details before placing an order.

4. Location Permission

Customers may voluntarily use the “Get Current Location” option during checkout.

Location access helps capture latitude and longitude for delivery assistance.

Location permission does not replace the need to enter a complete delivery address.

5. Estimated Delivery Time

The estimated delivery time may normally be shown during ordering or communicated after order confirmation.

Delivery time may vary depending on:

  • Food preparation time
  • Number of items ordered
  • Current order volume
  • Delivery distance
  • Traffic conditions
  • Weather conditions
  • Road closures
  • Address accuracy
  • Unexpected operational issues

Delivery times are estimates and should not be treated as guaranteed arrival times.

6. Order Preparation

Food preparation begins after an order is accepted or confirmed.

Preparation time may vary based on the selected food items, quantity and current kitchen workload.

7. Customer Availability

Customers should remain reachable through the mobile number provided during checkout.

Delivery personnel may contact the customer to:

  • Confirm the address
  • Request directions
  • Verify a landmark
  • Inform the customer about arrival
  • Resolve delivery-access issues

8. Failed Delivery

Delivery may fail when:

  • The address is incorrect
  • The address is incomplete
  • The mobile number is incorrect
  • The customer cannot be contacted
  • The customer is unavailable
  • The building or location cannot be accessed
  • The delivery area is unsupported
  • The customer refuses the confirmed order

Refund or replacement may not be available when delivery fails because of incorrect customer information or customer unavailability.

9. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges, when applicable, may depend on:

  • Delivery distance
  • Order value
  • Delivery area
  • Current business offers
  • Special delivery requirements

Applicable delivery charges should be shown before the customer confirms the order.

10. Cash on Delivery

Customers selecting Cash on Delivery should keep the payable amount ready when the order arrives.

Digital payment at delivery may be accepted only when an authorised payment option is available.

11. Contactless Delivery

Contactless delivery may be provided when requested and operationally possible.

Customers should provide clear instructions and ensure that the delivery location is safe and accessible.

12. Order Inspection

Customers should check the delivered package promptly.

Report missing items, wrong items, damaged packaging or significant spillage as soon as reasonably possible.

Supporting details may include:

  • Order ID
  • Customer name or phone number
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the package

13. Delivery Delays

We will make reasonable efforts to complete deliveries within the estimated time.

Delays caused by traffic, weather, preparation time, order volume or events beyond reasonable control may occur.

Minor delivery delays do not automatically qualify for cancellation or refund.

14. Severe Weather and Emergencies

Deliveries may be delayed, restricted or cancelled during severe weather, flooding, road closures, local restrictions, emergencies or other unsafe conditions.

Customer and delivery-person safety will be prioritised in such circumstances.

15. Bulk and Special Orders

Large, bulk, party or special orders may require advance confirmation and additional preparation time.

Delivery time, charges and payment terms for such orders may be communicated separately.

16. Order Status

Customers may view available order-status updates through the My Orders page.

Possible order statuses may include:

  • Pending
  • Confirmed
  • Preparing
  • Out for Delivery
  • Delivered
  • Cancelled

17. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel eligible orders while the status remains Pending.

Cancellation may not be available once food preparation has started or the order status has changed.

Read Cancellation & Refund Policy

18. Customer Support

Customers may contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods for delivery questions, address corrections, missing items or order concerns.

Address corrections should be requested before the order is dispatched.

19. Contact Us

For delivery-related assistance, contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods through our Contact page.
