These Terms and Conditions explain the rules for using the Sri Laxmi Home Foods website and food-ordering services. By accessing the website, creating an account or placing an order, you agree to follow these terms.

1. About Our Services

Sri Laxmi Home Foods provides homemade food ordering and delivery services. Our website allows customers to view available food items, create an account, add items to the cart and place delivery orders.

Delivery is currently available only within supported areas of Hyderabad.

2. Customer Accounts

Customers may need to register and login before placing an order. You are responsible for providing accurate account information.

  • Provide a valid name, email address and mobile number.
  • Keep your login information private.
  • Do not allow unauthorised persons to use your account.
  • Inform us when you suspect unauthorised account activity.

We may restrict or disable accounts used for fraudulent, abusive or unlawful activity.

3. Food Information

We aim to display accurate information about food names, prices, descriptions, ingredients and availability.

Food appearance, portion size and presentation may vary slightly from website photographs. Images may be used for representation purposes.

4. Food Availability

All food items are subject to availability. An item displayed on the website may become unavailable because of ingredient shortages, order volume or preparation limitations.

We may contact the customer when an ordered item is unavailable and may offer a replacement, modification or cancellation.

5. Order Placement

Before placing an order, customers should verify:

  • Selected food items
  • Quantity of each item
  • Delivery address
  • Mobile number
  • Hyderabad pincode
  • Selected payment method

Submitting an order request does not always guarantee acceptance. An order may be rejected when an item is unavailable, the address is outside the delivery area or the supplied information is incomplete.

6. Prices

Food prices are displayed in Indian Rupees. Prices may change based on ingredient costs, availability, offers or business requirements.

The price displayed when the order is confirmed will normally apply to that order, unless an obvious technical or pricing error has occurred.

7. Payment

Cash on Delivery is currently available. Online payment may be introduced later through an authorised third-party payment provider.

Customers should never share card PINs, UPI PINs, bank passwords or OTPs with delivery persons or Sri Laxmi Home Foods staff.

8. Delivery

Delivery times shown on the website are estimates. Actual delivery time may vary because of:

  • Food preparation time
  • Order volume
  • Traffic conditions
  • Weather conditions
  • Address accuracy
  • Delivery distance
  • Unexpected operational issues

Customers should provide a complete and reachable delivery address and remain available through the registered mobile number.

9. Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for entering the correct house number, street, area, landmark, city and pincode.

Sri Laxmi Home Foods may not be responsible for delays or failed delivery caused by an incorrect, incomplete or unreachable address.

10. Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order while its status is still marked as Pending.

Once preparation begins or the order status is changed to Confirmed, Preparing, Out for Delivery or Delivered, cancellation may not be available.

Additional details are provided in our Cancellation and Refund Policy.

11. Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the reason for cancellation, payment method, food condition and order status.

Cash on Delivery orders normally do not involve an advance payment. Online-payment refunds, when introduced, may require processing time from the payment provider or bank.
