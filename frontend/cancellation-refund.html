Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This Cancellation and Refund Policy explains when a customer may cancel an order and when a refund or replacement may be considered by Sri Laxmi Home Foods.

1. Customer Order Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order through the My Orders page while the order status is:

  • Pending

A Cancel Order button will be displayed only for eligible Pending orders.

2. Orders That Cannot Be Cancelled

Cancellation may no longer be available when the order status has changed to:

  • Confirmed
  • Preparing
  • Out for Delivery
  • Delivered
  • Already Cancelled

This is because ingredients may have been used, food preparation may have started or the order may already be assigned for delivery.

3. Cancellation by Sri Laxmi Home Foods

Sri Laxmi Home Foods may cancel an order when:

  • A selected food item is unavailable
  • Ingredients are unavailable
  • The delivery address is outside the supported Hyderabad service area
  • Customer details are incorrect or incomplete
  • The customer cannot be contacted
  • There is a technical or pricing error
  • Unexpected operational conditions prevent food preparation or delivery
  • Fraudulent or suspicious order activity is identified

We will make reasonable efforts to inform the customer when an order is cancelled by us.

4. Cash on Delivery Orders

Cash on Delivery orders do not normally require an advance payment. Therefore, no payment refund is required when an eligible Cash on Delivery order is cancelled before delivery.

Repeated refusal of valid Cash on Delivery orders may result in restrictions on future orders.

5. Online Payment Refunds

Online payment functionality may be introduced later through an authorised payment provider.

When an eligible prepaid order is cancelled, an approved refund will normally be returned to the original payment method.

The time required for the refund to appear may depend on the payment provider, bank, UPI service or card issuer.

6. Wrong Food Item

When a customer receives a food item different from the confirmed order, the customer should contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods as soon as possible.

Please provide:

  • Order ID
  • Registered name or phone number
  • Description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the received item, when reasonably possible

After verification, we may offer a replacement, partial refund, full refund or another suitable resolution.

7. Missing Food Item

When an item is missing from an order, contact us promptly with the Order ID and details of the missing item.

After verification, we may arrange delivery of the missing item, issue an eligible refund or provide another appropriate resolution.

8. Damaged or Spilled Order

When food packaging is damaged, opened or significantly spilled during delivery, contact us promptly and provide photographs when reasonably possible.

The issue will be reviewed before a replacement or refund is approved.

9. Food Quality Concerns

Customers should contact us promptly when they believe that delivered food has a genuine quality or safety issue.

We may request the Order ID, photographs, packaging details and a clear description of the concern to investigate the matter.

Personal taste preferences, spice expectations or minor differences in presentation may not automatically qualify for a refund.

10. Incorrect Customer Information

A refund or replacement may not be available when delivery fails because the customer provided:

  • An incorrect address
  • An incomplete address
  • An incorrect mobile number
  • An unsupported delivery pincode
  • No response to reasonable delivery calls

Customers should verify all delivery details before placing an order.

11. Delivery Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Minor delays caused by preparation time, order volume, traffic, weather or unexpected operational conditions may not automatically qualify for a refund.

Significant delays will be reviewed based on the circumstances of the order.

12. Partially Consumed Food

Refunds may not normally be approved when most of the food has already been consumed, unless there is a verified food-quality or safety concern.

13. Duplicate Payment

When online payments are introduced, customers should contact us if the same order is charged more than once.

After verification with the payment provider, an eligible duplicate payment will be processed for refund.

14. Refund Review

Every refund request may be reviewed using:

  • Order status
  • Payment status
  • Food preparation status
  • Delivery information
  • Customer communication
  • Photographs or supporting evidence

Approval depends on the facts and circumstances of the specific order.

15. Refund Method

Approved online-payment refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method, where supported.

Sri Laxmi Home Foods will not request a customer’s UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card PIN, bank password or OTP to process a refund.

16. How to Raise a Request

To report a cancellation, missing item, wrong item, damaged order or refund concern, contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods through the Contact page.

Include the Order ID and a clear explanation so that the issue can be reviewed quickly.

17. Contact Us

Contact Sri Laxmi Home Foods for cancellation, refund, replacement or order-related support.
