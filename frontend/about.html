OUR STORY

Bringing Home-Cooked Taste to Your Doorstep

Sri Laxmi Home Foods was started with a simple purpose: to provide fresh, tasty and hygienic homemade food to people who miss traditional home cooking.

Every item is prepared using carefully selected ingredients, authentic spices and trusted family recipes.

Our menu includes breakfast items, biryani, traditional meals, snacks, sweets, pickles and refreshing drinks.
